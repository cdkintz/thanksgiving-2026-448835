Mashed potatoes 🥔

Dairy-free. Start at 2:30, done by about 4:00, served at 5:00. Tap each step when it's done.

Never use a blender, food processor or the whisk. They turn potatoes into glue. Use the stand mixer's flat paddle (the cake beater) on low only.

What you need

  • Yukon Gold potatoes10 lb bag
  • Garlic confit (mason jar in fridge)whole jar
  • Chicken stock, hot2 to 3 cups
  • Salt for the water~2 tbsp per gallon
  • Salt and pepper to seasonto taste

Steps

2:30

Peel all the potatoes and cut into even 2 inch chunks.

2:50

Put the chunks in a bowl, cover with cold water and swirl. Drain. Do it twice.

2:55

Into the big pot. Cover with cold water by an inch. Salt it well (about 2 tbsp per gallon; it should taste like the sea). High heat.

~3:20

When it boils, turn down to a gentle simmer. Simmer 15 to 20 min.

While it simmers

Put the garlic confit jar contents in a small pot on low to melt. Mash the soft garlic into the fat with a fork. Heat 3 cups stock in another pot.

~3:40

Check: a knife slides into a chunk with no resistance. If not, give it a few more minutes.

Drain

Drain in a colander. Rinse under hot water for 30 seconds. Let them sit 1 minute to steam dry.

Mix

Potatoes into the stand mixer bowl. Flat paddle, lowest speed, 30 to 60 seconds, just until mostly smooth. Stop early; a few small lumps are fine.

Fat first

On low, add the garlic fat in 3 parts. Mix a few seconds after each.

Then stock

Add the hot stock about a cup at a time, mixing on low a few seconds each, until creamy and soft. Stop when it holds soft peaks; it firms up as it sits.

Season

Taste. Add salt and pepper a little at a time until it tastes great.

Hold till 5:00

Scrape into the pot or a heatproof bowl. Press foil onto the surface and cover. Keep warm over a pot of barely simmering water, or in the oven at 200°F with the stuffings. Stir every 20 min. Not on a direct burner.

4:55

Stir in a splash of hot stock to loosen. Into the serving bowl.
